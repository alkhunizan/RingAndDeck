## Elgato: Published

[AI Productivity Workflows](https://marketplace.elgato.com/product/ai-productivity-workflows-78656753-93f5-4c48-a0c6-37a6c2e40d1d) is live and Free on Elgato Marketplace under [Aziz Al Khunizan / @azizme](https://marketplace.elgato.com/@azizme).

Approved 25 September and published 30 September 2026 as marketplace version 0.1. The collection contains Code Workflow, AI Workflow and Browser Workflow from alpha.3, targeting Windows and the 15-key Stream Deck. The public page has a Download button, one thumbnail, three generated-layout gallery previews, setup, support, source and privacy links. Generated layouts remain labeled separately from native screenshots; alpha status and testing limitations remain visible.

The public creator profile has a portrait, a Saudi AI engineer bio, GitHub, website and support links. Marketplace acceptance is a product approval, not partner certification or complete device validation. No paid monetization was enabled.

## Logitech: not submitted

Logitech support confirmed on 24 September 2026 that universal `.lp5` profiles are not currently supported. Its profile-sharing route accepts profiles tied to specific applications. The universal Code Ring, Work Ring and Flow Ring profiles remain available free through the [GitHub alpha.3 release](https://github.com/alkhunizan/RingAndDeck/releases/tag/v0.1.0-alpha.3).

The signed-in [contribution form](https://marketplace.logi.com/contribute) accepts `.lplug4` plugins. A genuine separately compiled Ring & Deck Preview plugin candidate is installed locally. It has not been uploaded, submitted, approved or published. The standalone profiles have not been renamed or disguised as plugins.

Before submission, complete supported-hardware runtime tests, owner review of the Developer EULA and owner acceptance of the Developer Agreement. The [official approval guidelines](https://logitech.github.io/actions-sdk-docs/marketplace-approval-guidelines/) require hardware/software testing and a Developer EULA. The [Developer Agreement](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/legal/developer-agreement) identifies the first upload as an acceptance event; the owner handles legal acceptance personally.

The compiled candidate includes original project MIT code and Microsoft Fluent Emoji MIT artwork. It does not redistribute historical Lucide ISC artwork; the repository preserves the original ISC notices. This inventory does not assert vendor acceptance of ISC or replace Logitech's review. The distribution target remains Free/$0; no payment, payout or paid listing has been configured. Confirm the submission form's distribution terms before sending the candidate.
